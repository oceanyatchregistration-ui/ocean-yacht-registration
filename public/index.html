Which flag is right for my yacht?+

The right option depends on ownership, vessel specifications, intended use and cruising plans. Your adviser can help compare suitable jurisdictions before you apply.

What information should I have ready?+

Start with your yacht’s make, length, year, current registration and ownership details. The full document checklist depends on the selected registry and your circumstances.

Can I change ownership or modify an existing Polish registration?+

Yes. Change of ownership, modification of an existing Polish registration, deletion certificates and duplicate registration documentation are available. The exact documents depend on your vessel and current registration.

How long does the process take?+

Timing varies by jurisdiction and the completeness of your documents. A realistic timeline can be outlined once your vessel and chosen registration route have been reviewed.

Can you help with a commercial yacht?+

Commercial vessels can have additional requirements. Include your intended use in the enquiry so the registration scope can be assessed appropriately.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Useful answers
before you begin.

Does Polish registration need renewal?+

The service is presented as a lifetime Polish flag registration. Ocean Yacht Registration does not offer a registration-renewal service.

What vessel lengths can I configure online?+

The current pricing system supports vessels up to 24 metres, with separate base tiers for 0–7 m, 7.1–12 m and 12.1–24 m.

Can I apply for an ownership change or modification?+

Yes. The configured services include change of ownership, Polish registration modification, deletion certificate and duplicate registration.

Can I add a Polish MMSI radio licence?+

Yes. An optional Polish MMSI radio licence can be added to supported registration applications.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before
you register.

Clear answers to the practical questions around Polish yacht registration.

What information do I need to start?+

Your chosen service, vessel length, intended use, applicant details, yacht details and supporting documents.

Can I save my application and return later?+

Yes. Your secure draft remains available in the same browser while its draft session is active.

How are my documents handled?+

Files are stored privately and are available only through authorised application/admin routes.

How do I know my final price?+

The registration workspace calculates the price server-side from your selected service and options and shows the configured total before submission.

How do I track my application?+

Use your application reference together with the same email address used for the registration.

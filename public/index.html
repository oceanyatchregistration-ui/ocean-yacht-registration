FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you
begin.

Quick answers about the services and online application.

What do I need to start?+

Have your contact details, vessel information and supporting documents ready. Choose your service and options, then review the total before submitting. No payment is collected with the application.

What vessel lengths can I configure online?+

The current pricing system supports vessels up to 24 metres, with base tiers for 1–7 m, 7.1–12 m and 12.1–24 m.

Can I apply for an ownership change or modification?+

Yes. Available services include change of ownership, registration modification, deletion certificate and duplicate registration.

Can I add a Polish MMSI radio licence?+

Yes. A Polish MMSI radio licence can be selected as an optional add-on in the registration workspace.

How do I track my application?+

After submission, use your Ocean application reference together with the application email on the Track Registration page.
